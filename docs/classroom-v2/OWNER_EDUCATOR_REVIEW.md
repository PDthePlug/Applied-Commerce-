# Term review and next-batch gate

This implemented term is ready for structured owner and educator review. Real authenticated-role checks remain NOT TESTED without isolated fixtures. Agent source review and schema validation are not independent educational approval. Record a named educator, review date, lesson identity, version, classroom context, observations and disposition before approving a teaching plan. Keep private examples and indicative answers within the protected content system.

For each lesson, review:

1. Whether a teacher can prepare from the opening disclosure and explain the main concept accurately at Grade 8 level.
2. Whether every teaching intervention actually sits beside the learner material it supports, including revisits, Part/Step headings and any recorded placement exception.
3. Whether the questions elicit reasoning and whether secure, partial and not-yet-evidenced understanding lead to a useful next teaching move.
4. Whether each activity has a feasible classroom, later or optional disposition and whether real observations remain distinct from predictions and simulations.
5. Whether support, extension, quiet participation, reading access, large classes and paper-based alternatives are useful in a real classroom.
6. Whether periods fit 45 minutes without rushing personal reflection, discussion or evidence review. Test both periods where planned; record observed rather than merely estimated timing.
7. Whether privacy, consent, essential needs and safety are protected, especially the source statements highlighted in the educational-quality register.
8. Whether private preparation, local detail and complete printing are usable without a second live full-length teacher document.
9. Whether learners retain independent access, stable saved answers and completion/portfolio behaviour.
10. Whether the protected staging educator and learner roles have been exercised with isolated accounts. Unavailable fixtures remain NOT TESTED, not an implied pass.

Use the lesson-specific coverage map to verify a missing detail before proposing deletion or consolidation. Source text changes require separate editorial review; they are not silently incorporated through this teacher layer. The private release and original source fingerprints identify exactly what was reviewed.

Do not begin the next grade-and-term batch until the owner and educators review this term and record the benchmark quality, placement, timing and access limitations. Subsequent work must again inspect each actual lesson, author distinct guidance, moderate its educational substance, verify source alignment and learner regression, and stage a small reviewable batch. Schema-valid generic prose is not a substitute for that process. The remaining 371 undeveloped lesson guides are not approved or generated by this milestone.
