# Applied Commerce classroom teaching standard V2

The owner-approved contextual interface in PR #43 is the reference architecture. This rollout delivers Grade 8 Term 1 for owner and educator review. It also adapts the four existing Grade 9–12 benchmarks to demonstrate preservation, variable stage counts and two-period delivery. It does not author another term or claim approval of all 395 guides.

The original learner lesson is the presentation material. Educator preparation opens at the beginning, collapsed by default. Guidance belongs beside an intact, verified source section or an actual standalone Part/Step heading. Its primary explanation, question, participation and understanding check remain compact; reasoning, misconceptions, scaffolds, support, extension and next teaching moves use a local disclosure. No full teaching document is appended to the live lesson.

## Instructional design and timing

A period has 45 minutes, but its number of stages and the sequence are chosen from the actual source. Plans may have one or two periods. A second period has its own retrieval and observable output; it is not a doubled first-period template. Explicit navigation follows delivery order even when it revisits earlier learner material. Preparation identifies prerequisites, objectives, observable outcome, materials, vocabulary, misconceptions, context and participation/access conditions.

Each teaching moment explains what to do, asks a lesson-specific question, describes learner participation, identifies evidence of understanding and offers a next move when reasoning is incomplete. A hypothetical case is identified as hypothetical. A scheduled action or filled response is not evidence of mastery, real-world behaviour or a successful outcome. Existing assessments, marks and completion rules are unchanged.

The first term uses 25 periods across 20 lessons. Lessons 1, 2, 3, 4 and 20 use two periods because of their maps, audits, agreement or portfolio workload. Lesson 4 retains the authored agreement's full 35-minute sequence within its first period. All other term lessons have one-period core plans. These are source-based planning judgements, pending classroom timing pilots.

Later observations, interviews, home tasks and optional reading have separate locations and zero contact-time minutes. A three-day audit, one-week saving trial or 24-hour needs/wants observation cannot be presented as completed during a 45-minute class. Tasks with later result fields retain those fields and their identities; teachers distinguish the plan from the result.

## Content accuracy, dignity and access

Read authored outcomes, vocabulary, explanations, activities and checkpoints before writing guidance. Never infer a lesson's intent from its title. Keep canonical text intact; register proposed source corrections separately. Explain the limits of motivational equations, financial examples, deterministic claims and personal labels. Check arithmetic and assumptions. Do not assert current product fees, eligibility or guaranteed returns without verified official information.

Provide a fictional/redacted route, private writing and the right to pass on public sharing. Saved digital work may be reviewed by authorised staff; never promise otherwise. Protect essential needs, safety, consent and realistic capacity. Do not require money, purchases, loans, unsafe contact, hazardous experiments or household financial disclosures. Income, public confidence and particular values are not mastery criteria.

Use paper/board equivalents and short reading chunks. Permit familiar-language rehearsal while retaining subject vocabulary. Large classes use paired rehearsal, rotating roles and individual explanations sampled by the teacher. Support models one reasoning step; extension changes a condition or tests a limit rather than adding repetitive writing. Independent learners retain the same original lesson and response flow.

## Coverage and alignment contract

The encrypted V2 release separates instructional text fields from their layout. Each field has one or more preparation, contextual or later-learning locations. Orphans fail validation. Titles and transitions count as instructional fields. Printing is a second complete representation, never the sole surviving location. Original benchmark fields are checked individually against the decrypted original release, including all 100 Grade 8 fields and 24 contextual clarifications.

Each insertion specifies an actual section/heading, edge, supporting block references and review rationale. A source reference alone does not establish a visible insertion point. The release binds to the resolved runtime unit fingerprint, not an inferred title or source offset. Automated static rendering must invoke every reviewed insertion exactly once. Lists, tables, grouped insight content and response indices are not split or renumbered.

Schema, timing, hash and browser checks establish technical properties. They do not approve instructional quality. Source review, agent judgement, independent educator moderation, classroom pilot and owner acceptance remain distinct evidence categories.

## Security, printing and release

Only the server-authorised educator API can decrypt teaching text. It verifies identity, active session and existing educator/admin authority before importing the private library. Mode flags, URLs and public availability metadata grant no access. Private responses are no-store and bound to the expected account. Sign-out, account/session/lesson changes and revalidation drop private state. No teacher text is stored in learner local storage or a public offline package.

Educators share a separate learner presentation window: blank disabled original response fields, no private guidance request, no saved answers and no claim of device synchronisation. Private printing mounts a complete print-only guide for the dialog and removes it afterwards. The existing standalone original benchmark guides remain available as a fallback.

Deliver reviewed, encrypted term batches through draft PRs and isolated staging. Require owner and educator review of this term before another batch. No production database writes, migrations, canonical-book rewrites, learner-identity changes, production deployment or merge are part of this milestone.
