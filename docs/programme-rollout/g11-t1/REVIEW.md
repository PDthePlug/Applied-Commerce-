Twenty authored lessons recommend **32 provisional 45-minute contacts**: 31 for the new nineteen plus the unchanged one-period benchmark. This is based on reading, individual writing, calculations, feedback, project revision and presentations, not teacher-guidance length. Each two-period guide provides a brief pause/reintroduction that continues the same learner lesson. Detailed individual rationales and source hashes are in [individual-review.json](individual-review.json).

Presentation timing assumes thirty learners, split fifteen per lesson, with about three minutes to present and one minute for response/transition. Each learner presents once. The fixed source tables describe workbook characters, not a new row or written quota for every classmate. Actual enrolment, reading/writing pace and access needs require educator timing trials and may change contacts.

Week-long trials, interviews, actual teaching to another person and real-world first steps are later work. Classroom checkpoints use original work and reasons; future results are never invented. Personal reflection remains; learners may choose suitable details and need not read private answers to classmates. Authorised educators/institution administrators can review saved digital evidence within their permissions.

[Focused source proposals](editorial-proposals.json) remain separate and unapplied. The guide provides necessary local clarification/safeguards while retaining legitimate lever/architect/shadow metaphors and the workbook’s voice. Technical validity and exact field counts do not establish classroom effectiveness or independent educator approval.
