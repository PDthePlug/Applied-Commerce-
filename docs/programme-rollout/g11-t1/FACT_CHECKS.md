# Source and arithmetic review

The actual learner lessons were read individually. Twenty-four focused proposals are recorded in [editorial-proposals.json](editorial-proposals.json), with exact passages, concerns, proposed wording and implementation impact. No source correction is applied. The accepted Lesson 14 benchmark is unchanged; its earlier conceptual concerns remain in the separate existing register.

Checked calculations:

- Lesson 5: Option A R11,000 / 22%; Option B R1,250 monthly interest, R750 monthly after-interest operating profit, R9,000 annual operating profit and R20,000 capital gain, totalling R29,000 / 58%. The falling-value case is R9,000 − R40,000 = −R31,000 / −62%. The source uses a simple interest-only model excluding other costs; Part B contains the loss calculation.
- Lesson 6: debt R480,000; R500 monthly margin before other costs; assumed 6% growth gives R636,000. Unchanged debt would imply R156,000 equity, but exact after-year equity requires a remaining loan balance. R120,000 at R500 monthly without interest is 240 months / 20 years. No new amortisation exercise is required.
- Lesson 7: the source uses total investment / own contribution. Shared partner capital is not equivalent to borrowed debt or entitlement to all business returns. Ratios cannot be calculated for the five cases without missing amounts.
- Lesson 8: independent +20% and −30% asset moves at 2:1 give +40% and −60% gross equity changes. Sequential moves from R10,000 give R12,000 then R8,400, leaving R3,400 equity against R5,000 initial equity: −32%. Equal-sized market moves have equal gross multipliers. The source’s R2,000/R3,000 loss references conflict; it also supplies no formal risk-adjusted-return calculation.
- Lesson 10: on the simplified assumptions, R2,000 + R800 − (10 × R50) = R2,300. Extra earnings are assumed and further costs are omitted.

Interest/growth ranges, app availability/terms, taxation, labour/contract/UIF requirements are not certified as current offers or legal advice. The register distinguishes missing assumptions, specific activity safeguards and current-reference questions from material arithmetic errors. Property ownership cannot be inferred solely from patience; a decision not to borrow can be sound. These qualifications are local to the relevant work and do not become corrective lectures after every paragraph.

Lever, architect, shadow and growth stories retain their educational purpose. Individual reflection, knowledge routines and future-self writing remain. The scale is treated as reflection with examples, not a validated achievement score. The later delegation/deletion/sharing tasks have specific consent/safety/privacy alternatives alongside the task; no new compulsory fictional exercises are added.
