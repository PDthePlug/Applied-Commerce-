# Focused factual verification

The original learner text remains unchanged. These checks support the separate editorial proposals and local teacher clarification; they do not turn the lessons' metaphors into scientific lectures.

- [Lally and colleagues, “How are habits formed”](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674): the [publisher-supplied Crossref abstract](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1002/ejsp.674) was retrieved on 23 October 2026. Its study reports considerable variation in modelled automaticity times and no material effect from one missed opportunity. This supports avoiding a universal 21-day or fifty-repetition deadline. The study's estimates are not a prescription for an individual learner; its twelve-week observational design and modelled extrapolation also matter.
- [Official Basic Conditions of Employment Act page](https://www.gov.za/documents/basic-conditions-employment-act) and [original Act PDF](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/a75-97.pdf), retrieved on the same date: section 43 restricts employment below 15/minimum school-leaving age and employment that is age-inappropriate or risks a child's wellbeing, education or development. The Act defines a child as under 18. Do not infer that every safe ordinary learning activity is employment. The government page links an amended text updated to January 2020; that consolidated source returned HTTP 403. A complete current legal/exception review remains pending, so teacher guidance uses safe planning alternatives and applicable school/work rules without asserting a blanket legal conclusion.

Retrieval limitations: Firecrawl had no credits and did not execute the research requests; backup web search returned an error; two attempted UCL news addresses returned 404. The DOI abstract and official government page/PDF were retrieved directly. These are separate from the passing staging network, authentication and application-permission checks. No key, session or private teaching prose was sent to a research service.
