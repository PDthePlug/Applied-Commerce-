## Lesson 65: SASSA telephone-verification story

[SASSA, 28 July 2026: beneficiaries whose grants are under review](https://sassa.gov.za/documents/uploads/IMPORTANT_MESSAGE_FOR_SASSA_BENEFICIARIES_WHOSE_GRANTS_ARE_UNDER_REVIEW.pdf) directs the affected beneficiaries to attend review appointments with the requested documents. It says beneficiaries who are bedridden or medically unable to visit should contact the nearest office; an authorised representative may be appointed where applicable. It also encourages use of available digital/online services.

[SASSA, 29 September 2026: grant assessments and social-grant reviews](https://sassa.gov.za/documents/uploads/Media_Statement2.pdf) distinguishes an eligibility assessment from a subsequent review, stresses the relevant grant’s requirements and requests that beneficiaries respond to their review notifications. It describes verification using relevant information with the appropriate legal basis and consent.

**Finding:** these are relevant official sources, but neither establishes a universal rule that an ordinary review can be completed over the phone by answering security questions. The story’s specific outcome remains unverified as current general procedure; these sources do not prove that every possible remote arrangement is impossible. Keep the story separate from procedural advice. Existing local teacher support directs actual matters to appropriate responsible support and independently verified official channels, without learners handling identity/security records.
