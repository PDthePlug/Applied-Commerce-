# Provisional contact-time recommendations

Retain **20 authored Grade 8 Term 1 lessons**. Recommend **25 contact periods of 45 minutes** (1,125 minutes), provisionally, until educator timing trials. The five lessons below each retain two periods because of learner work, not guide length. The other fifteen have one-period core plans; later tasks do not count as contact time. The four higher-grade benchmarks add six periods, making 31 across the existing 24 plans.

| Lesson | Period 1 learning work and observable outcome | Period 2 learning work and observable outcome | Suitable later work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Who Am I? | Story/concept reading and private reflection about 20–25 min; seven-circle present map about 18–20 min. Learner can identify influences in their map. | Reopen map, create future comparison and one specific action about 15 min; truth/wish audit about 14 min; deeper reflection/checkpoint about 16 min. Learner distinguishes a belief, a wish, available resources and a feasible action. | Trusted-person word/prediction and real observation. No interview manufactured in class. |
| 2 Where Beliefs Come From | Read and reflect, complete Activity 3 and six-person inventory with honest unknowns. Allow about 20 min reading/reflection, 10 min sentence stems and 15 min inventory. Outcome: distinguish stated, observed and inferred origins. | Retrieve inventory; allow about 20 min cost audit, 10 min evidence-informed reconsideration and 15 min checkpoint/prediction. Outcome: explain one belief/choice/cost with constraints and a safe plan. | Consensual family conversation or clearly labelled hypothesis. Actual experiment results later. |
| 3 Personal Efficacy | Read Lerato and reflect about 15 min; ten capability statements about 20 min; genuine observed feedback/planning about 10 min. Outcome: distinguish confidence, evidence and unobserved capability. | Retrieve practice/skill distinction about 5 min; transfer mapping about 20 min; reflection/checkpoint about 20 min. Outcome: link skill to another task with a reason and supported next attempt. | External observer where not available; safe contribution/offer. No required earnings or invented validation. |
| 4 How Others Shape Us | About 10 min introduction and the authored agreement sequence of **5 + 10 + 15 + 5 = 35 min**. Outcome: workable shared behaviours and a review point. | Reintroduce agreement and read influence material about 10 min; map all three circles and belief rows about 20 min; reflection/checkpoint about 15 min. Outcome: explain an influence and a safe question without deterministic labels. | Optional acknowledgement/ordinary decision or authored imagined alternative. No confrontation required. |
| 20 Term Review and Portfolio | About 7 min retrieval; 10 min equation usefulness/limits; 18 min seven self-assessment questions; 10 min selection/review of existing evidence. Outcome: supported claim about learning, with uncertainty. | Reopen evidence; about 15 min portfolio entry, 18 min future letter, 12 min final reflection/checkpoint. Outcome: curated work, justified growth claim, question and goal for Term 2. | Finish or privately decorate the letter; voluntary exhibition/feedback. Do not require a public reading. |

Brief first-period conclusion: keep the current map, inventory, capability list, agreement or selected evidence, and identify where the same lesson resumes. Brief second-period reintroduction: reopen that work, recall one relevant idea and continue the next original activity. The live interface now provides these continuation cues; it does not create another learner lesson or completion record.

Trial protocol: record actual reading, thinking, writing, negotiation, feedback and transition times; sample readers needing support as well as fluent readers. Ask whether essential work was rushed or displaced to homework. Reallocate stage minutes within the provisional envelope before proposing more or fewer contact periods. In particular, the existing L4 plan overallocates a rebuttal of the average claim; reclaim that time for all circle-map rows and individual thinking.
